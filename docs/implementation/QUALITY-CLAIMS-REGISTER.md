## Compliance rules applied

- Form-25 is treated as a manufacturing licence, never a certification.
- A Form-25 licence reference is not equated with WHO-GMP status.
- WHO-GMP is published only for Siflon Pharma, where a certificate is on file.
- No ISO, GMP, FDA, CDSCO or other certification is asserted for Siflon Drugs, Siflon Polymers or Siflon Pipes.
- Expired or unverified certificates are not presented as current.
- No certificate numbers, issuing authorities or validity dates have been invented.
- Siflon Pipes remains pending.
- Records are not shared across divisions.
