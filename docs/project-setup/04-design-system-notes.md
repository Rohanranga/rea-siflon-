# Siflon Group Website - Design System & Component Notes

## Design Philosophy
The Siflon Group website embodies a premier, high-end industrial and life sciences aesthetic. It balances clinical precision with modern digital interactivity.

## Color Palette & Styling
- **Primary**: Deep industrial slate and navy tones (`#0f172a`, `#1e293b`)
- **Accent**: Vibrant life-science cyan and emerald accents
- **Typography**: Inter sans-serif font family with proportional scaling for hero titles and section headers
- **Borders & Glassmorphism**: Subtle translucent borders (`border-slate-800/50`) with backdrop blur

## Core Components
1. **Hero Component (`hero.tsx`)**: High-impact introductory section with dynamic value propositions and call-to-actions.
2. **Business Selector & Tilt Stack (`business-selector.tsx`, `business-ttilt-stack.tsx`)**: Interactive 3D tilt cards showcasing Siflon's diversified business divisions.
3. **Exhibition Gallery (`exhibition-gallery.tsx`)**: Immersive grid and lightbox view of global exhibitions and manufacturing plants.
4. **Industries Marquee (`industries-marquee.tsx`)**: Infinite scrolling banner highlighting served industry verticals.
5. **Contact Form (`contact-form.tsx`)**: Accessible, validated lead generation and inquiry form.
